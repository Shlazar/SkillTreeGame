Explicit human approval

Seven resolved design questions

Only these seven are labelled human-approved. The remaining tuning below is implementation choice or a design value.

  1. Remove Viper overheating: continuous fire and no Cooling node.
  2. Keep five existing cars: MG turrets on boxcar index 3, Katyusha on flatcar index 2 replacing its riders.
  3. Retain the short won/lost summary before returning to the Depot.
  4. Keep large readable hordes and reduce fractional kill payouts to meet the per-leg scrap targets.
  5. Cap extra one-time Gold Hunt rewards at five gold across the demo.
  6. Reveal gold on the first actual leg-3 gold reward, including arrival stars if the golden zombie escapes, and open the leg-2 chest then.
  7. Award remaining missed rescues at the final Terminus camp, including rescue events that never spawned, so no survivor is permanently lost.
